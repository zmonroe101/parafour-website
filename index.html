Why Parafour Innovations

We don't just meet standards — we helped write them.

✓

MET-Listed Manufacturing

Every P4 Series dispenser carries the MET mark, independently certified to ANSI/UL standards for LPG dispensing equipment used in commercial and fleet applications.

⚖

NTEP Category 1 Certified

Our dispensers hold NTEP Certificate of Conformance — the highest weights-and-measures certification for commercial fuel sales, legal for trade across all 50 states.

🔒

NFPA & NEC Compliant

Designed and built in strict compliance with NFPA 58 (LP-Gas Code), NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code), and NEC Article 514 — the codes that govern every LPG fueling installation.

★

Standards Leadership

Parafour's founder has been instrumental in shaping ASME, NFPA, and NTEP standards for LPG dispensing — giving our customers equipment designed by the people who set the bar.
